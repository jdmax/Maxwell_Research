  - So **Hopewell** is where the McClintock and Morrow families were in the early 1750s. That makes it a candidate for where the Maxwells lived before Tyrone, but no Maxwell appears on its surviving lists.

**4. Full-text searches for Sarah and for a widow (task d)**
- "Sarah Maxwell" OR "Sarah Maxwel" OR "Maxwell Sarah" OR "Maxwel Sarah" OR "Sarah Maxfield", Cumberland, 1750–1790: 9 hits.
  - None is the 1763 Sarah. They include Sarah Maxwell of Lurgan's will (1830), Sarah Maxwell the executrix of William Maxwell of Rye (about 1812), and Sarah Maxwell who married Samuel Test in 1796.
  - The 1763 tax entry itself did not show up in these results.
- "widow Maxwell" OR "Maxwell widow" OR the Maxfield variants, 1750–1790: 7 hits. Two are new and real:
  - **Rye 1779** (Taxpayer Lists 1776, 1779–1780, image 289, ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS7S-SK84). Read on the image, it has two separate Maxwell entries on the same list:
    > 100 | Maxwell Albert | 2 Horses | 2 Cows | … 144
    >
    > 100 | Maxwell Widow | 1 Horse | 1 Cow | … 150
  - **Rye 1780** (image 494, ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS7S-S2LN), read on the image:
    > 200 | Maxwell Widow | … 360 | 360
    - Albert is not on the 1780 M pages.
  - What it shows: **a Maxwell widow held land in Rye Twp in 1779–80, separately from Albert.** The obvious candidate is **Andrew's widow** (Albert administered Andrew's estate in 1772). Sarah is also possible, since Rye adjoins Tyrone and Sarah left the Tyrone/Toboyne list after 1763. Nothing names her. **Possible.**

**5. Land Office warrants, 1762 (task e)**
- Record: Cumberland Warrants (loose warrants, 1957 film).
  - Image 309, ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSY3-13HW-F: 8 June 1762. James Maxwell, 100 acres "in the Great Cove adjoining Samuel Brown's Improvement". Read on the image.
  - Image 307, ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSY3-13FW-5: 8 June 1762. **Patrick** Maxwell, 150 acres "in the great Cove adjoining the Tract allowed to James Maxwell and Abraham Stack's Improvement".
  - Image 311, ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSY3-13F3-J: 8 June 1762. James Maxwell, 150 acres "on Juniata at a place called Turkey Bottom adjoining and below Colonel James Burd's lands". The place wording is partly from OCR.
- Register: Cumberland warrant register, image 59, entries 171–173 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CSPG-M9RX-Q):
  > 171 Maxwell, Patrick | 150 | In Great Cove
  >
  > 172 " James | 100 | [Great Cove] | returned 11 March 1789 | patentee James Maxwell
  >
  > 173 " " | 150 | On Juniata R.
- Which Maxwell: James and Patrick applied together on the same day, and Great Cove lies beside Peters. That makes these **James Esq. and Patrick of the Peters family**, which your rules exclude. **Probable.**
- **No Maxwell warrant or application in Tyrone or Sherman's Valley, 1755–1766, was found.** James of Toboyne's "warranted" acreage in 1768 still can't be traced to a warrant. It may have been a location or an improvement right under the 1766 "New Purchase" or an East/West Side application. I didn't examine the 1765–69 East/West Side register page by page; the only Maxwell there was a "W. Maxwell" adjoiner in the Aughwick/Conococheague area.
