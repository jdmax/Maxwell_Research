### Findings

#### 1. John L. Maxwell household, 1850 and 1860, Butler Co.
- Proposed ID: John L. Maxwell (b. c.1790), son of William of Unity per your working model (the 1815 will names "John L."; the 1850 census name matches).
- Record: Butler Co., US census 1850, North Butler, dwelling/family 179, line 39, ARK 1:1:M44R-LW6. US census 1860, Butler Twp., p. 22, ARK 1:1:MXPD-GGJ (NARA M653).
- Date / place: 1850, 1860; Butler Co., PA.
- Verbatim transcription: Not a transcription of the image. Index fields only. 1850: John L Maxwell 60 PA; Agnes 60; A Bennet 24; Adam 21; Emily 19; John Lowery 16; Lydia Frear 11 (all PA). 1860: John L Maxwell 69 PA; John Maxwell 26; Martha Maxwell 27.
- What it shows: In 1850, one household has three sons: A. Bennet (b. c.1826), Adam (b. c.1829), John Lowery (b. c.1834). The "Johnston [?]" in your notes is indexed as **John Lowery**. In 1860 John L. lives with John (26) and Martha (27). Wife Agnes is absent, so she presumably died or is elsewhere.
- Which Maxwell, and confidence: John L. of North Butler — Probable (as the son of William of Unity, resting on the name "John L." plus Butler residence; there is no direct source naming his father).

#### 2. Adam Maxwell (b. 1829), son of John L.

- Record: 1860 Butler Twp., p. 22, ARK 1:1:MXPD-GG8 (Adam 31, Maria 29, Mary E 4, Flora 3). 1870 Apollo, Armstrong Co., household 105, line 23, p. 13, ARK 1:1:MZL6-JLS. 1880 Clarinda, Page Co., Iowa (index: wife Marie; children Isaac, Charley, Flora, Mary), not opened. 1910 Lamar, Prowers Co., Colorado (Adam 80 and Marie 78 shown as parents of I. - Date / place: 1860–1910.
- Verbatim transcription: Index fields only. 1870: Adam Maxwell 41 PA, head; Mariah 40; Mary 14; Flora 12; Isaac 8; Charles 1; James McMath 18 (Scotland).
- What it shows: Adam is in the same household as John L. in 1850, and his wife and daughters match 1860, 1870, 1880 and 1910, so the chain is consistent across censuses. He moved from Butler to Apollo, Armstrong Co. (1870), then to Page Co., Iowa (1880), then to Colorado. He had two sons: Isaac L. (b. c.1862–63) and Charles (b. c.1869). In 1910 Isaac L. (47, wife Mabel) is head at Lamar; no children are listed with him. Charles appears at Lamar in 1910 (wife Alda) and in 1920 in Prowers Co. (index: parent Maria Maxwell; wife Alda T.), not opened.
- Which Maxwell, and confidence: Confirmed that 1850 → 1860 → 1870 is the same man (shared wife, children); Probable for the link to the 1880 and 1910 records (matching wife Marie and children's names).
- Tree leads only (not evidence): FS tree profiles give Adam 1829–1916 and Isaac Leslie 1862–1919.
