**4. James Max(f)ield/Maxwell of Toboyne in the tax lists, 1768–1774 (Tasks B and D)**
- Records:
  - Cumberland Taxpayer Lists 1762–1770: Toboyne 1768, p. 171, image 389 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS79-1S84-6); Toboyne 1769, p. 361, image 486 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS79-1S8M-F); Toboyne 1770, p. 552, image 587 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS79-1SZL-K).
  - Cumberland Taxpayer Lists 1771–1775, 1778: Toboyne 1774, p. 323, image 369 (ARK 3:1:3Q9M-CS7S-952F, image 369).
- Verbatim, with the columns in order (acres patented | warranted | cleared | name and stock | rent | rate | province rate | county rate):
  - 1768 "Toboyne rates": `| 120 | 10 | Maxfield James — 1 ho. 2 Cows 3 Sheep | 5 | 3 | 1.9.0 | 1.12.0 | free`
  - 1769 "Toboyne Township 1769": `100 | 10 | Maxfield James — 1 Horse 1 Cow | 5 | 3 | 1 | 1.3 | 4`
  - 1770 "Toboyne 1770": `100 | 15 | Maxfield James — 1 horse 1 Cow 2 Sheep | 5 | 3 | …`
  - 1774 "Toboyne Rates 1774": `100 | 15 | Maxwell James — 1 horse 2 Cows 2 Sheep | 5 | 3 | 1.8.8 | 1.11.3 | 6`
- What it shows: The same man appears every year, as a taxed **householder with 100–120 acres, not a freeman**. His neighbours on the lists (McClintock, McNair, Moor, Morrow, Mitchel Ross) stay the same from year to year. So James was of age and farming in Toboyne by **1768**, which puts his birth in **1747 or earlier**. A freeman "Jas Maxfield" in Guilford Twp in 1770 is a different man (see Finding 6).
- The word "free" in the 1768 county-rate column also appears against other taxpayers on the Tyrone 1768 pages, so it is some tax notation. It does not mean "freeman."
- Which Maxwell: James of Toboyne. **Probable.** The spelling differs, but the place, the acreage, and the position among the same neighbours all match.

**7. The 11 Feb 1791 Wallace release, re-opened (Task C)**
- ARK: **3:1:3Q9M-CSF1-PSX** is the complete ARK. I tested it directly and it resolves to Franklin Co. "Deeds, 1784–1883", **Film/DGS 008067880, image 298 of 338**.
- Residence clause: there is **none for the grantors**. The deed says only "Between James Wallace of Franklin County Fannet Township of the one part and Catharine Corran Francis and Joanna Elliot, James and Isabella Hill, Adam and Elizabeth Maxwell, William and Anne Cartey, of the other part". No county or township is given for Adam and Elizabeth.
- Acknowledgment: the deed was **not acknowledged. It was proved by a witness 24 years later.** Verbatim:
  > Franklin County ss. Before me the subscriber one of the Justices of the peace in and for said County appeared Thomas L. Dunn who being duly sworn according to Law doth say, that the name Thomas L. Dunn set as a witness to the within is of his own hand writing, that the deponent was present at the execution of said Indenture and did see Catharine Corran, Francis & Joanna Elliott, James & Isabella Hill, Adam Maxwell therein named sign seal and deliver the same as their act and Deed, and that this deponent did at the same time write his name thereto as a witness and did also see Elizabeth Dunn [?] and William Carney the other subscribing Witness sign their names thereto as such — and that the name of William & Ann Cartney was at the same time signed for and with the consent of said William & Ann who were then present. Sworn & subscribed before me the eighteenth day of May A.D. 1815 Archd. L. McCune [?] / Thomas L. Dunn / A true Copy taken from the original the 27th of November 1815. John Findlay Recorder
- What it shows: The witness names **Adam** signing but not Elizabeth. The deed went unrecorded until 1815 and was proved by a witness, which fits grantors who had died or moved away. The document still gives no location for this Adam. It stays **Possible**.

2. **Where did Andrew live?** Check the Tyrone, Rye, Hopewell, and Newton tax lists **1762–1767** (Taxpayer Lists 1762–1770, before image 383) for an Andrew Maxwell or Maxfield. I only reached 1768–1774.
3. **When did James start holding land in Toboyne?** James was taxed on 120 acres warranted in 1768, well before his 1787 warrant. Look for an earlier application or location in the PA Archives Land Office warrant register for Cumberland, Toboyne, 1762–1768.

5. **Col. John's birth year.** Check the Perry Co. church records for Toboyne and Madison (Centre Presbyterian, Blain Lutheran/Reformed) and Cumberland Presbytery records. Also look for Toboyne in the 1800 and 1810 census by browsing the images, since the index may have missed James and John. John's age bracket there would bound his birth.
